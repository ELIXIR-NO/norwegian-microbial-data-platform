## Terms Of Use

### **Norwegian Microbial Data Platform (NMDP)**

**Version 1.0 – [13. February 2026]**

### 1. Purpose of the Portal

The Norwegian Microbial Data Platform (NMDP) is a web-based discovery portal that enables users to search, explore, and access metadata related to microbial samples managed within [MetaTrack](https://www.metatrack.no/).

NMDP provides a searchable interface for selected microbial metadata to enhance discoverability and responsible reuse of data. NMDP does not serve as a primary data storage system.

All storage, access control, and primary data management are governed by the MetaTrack Terms of Use.

---

### 2. Scope of Data Presented

NMDP displays selected metadata describing microbial samples and associated contextual information. The portal may provide:

- Publicly available metadata
- Metadata visible under controlled access
- Links to datasets managed within MetaTrack

Access to full datasets, restricted metadata, or data management functionality requires authentication and is subject to the MetaTrack Terms of Use.
