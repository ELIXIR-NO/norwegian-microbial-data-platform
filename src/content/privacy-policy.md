## **Privacy Policy**

### **Norwegian Microbial Data Platform (NMDP)**

**Version 1.0 – [13. February 2026]**

### 1. General

The Norwegian Microbial Data Platform (NMDP) is a discovery portal providing searchable access to selected microbial metadata managed within MetaTrack.

NMDP does not require user registration and does not collect or store personal data for account creation or data management purposes.

---

### 2. Authentication via MetaTrack

Users who choose to manage data are redirected to MetaTrack for authentication and data handling.

All personal data related to user accounts, authentication, and data management are processed under the MetaTrack Privacy Policy.

---

### 3. Technical Logs

NMDP may process limited technical information automatically generated during access to the portal, such as IP address and server logs, strictly for:

- Ensuring system security
- Maintaining service availability
- Detecting misuse

Such information is not used to identify individual users unless required for security or legal reasons.

---

### 4. Cookies

NMDP uses only essential technical cookies necessary for basic functionality.

No tracking or profiling cookies are used.

---

### 5. Contact

Questions regarding privacy in NMDP may be directed to [support@elixir.no](mailto:support@elixir.no).

For questions related to user accounts or data management in MetaTrack, please refer to the [MetaTrack Privacy Policy](https://www.metatrack.no/privacy-policy).
